Optimizer

Best pre‑shrine stats and pre/post order. Over budget? It drops the talents with the worst value per point.

Press Optimize build after moving it. Toward damage: weapon and attunement go up toward 100 (paid for by the least useful non‑damage talents) and open slots get damage talents. Toward talent options: weapon and attunement come down (never below what your weapon needs) and the points go to more talents. The middle keeps your build as it is.

Mantras

Each normal mantra costs 2 talent slots. Oath, monster and event mantras are free.

⚔ True combos

Starter mantras that hold the target (grab, bind or freeze, knock up or down, counter) and the follow‑ups fast enough to land after them, using the mantras this build has or can learn. Timings shift with ping, modifiers and patches, so test them in game and save the ones that work as your own.

    Equipment

    Click the search bar to see every outfit and item, strongest first. Type to filter by name, a talent it gives (try “orbital ice” or “worshipper”), or a slot. Click one and it goes into its slot under “Your armor”. Stars add pips; click a pip to switch it between health and armor.

    Your armor

    Health

    Optimize equipment reaches the minimum health and minimum physical armor first (500 HP and 50% by default), then spends the rest on health or physical armor by the slider. Elemental armor is never used, and Transfusion Ring is never picked.
