## v0.36 | 2026-11-17
- Every preset now has variations to pick from, like Attunementless: open a preset and choose a build shell at the top (for example High Strength: Strength 100 + Agility 40, Strength 100 + Agility 65 for Cheap Shot, or Strength 90 + Charisma 40). The first one is the preset as before.

- Diluvian can now start as a caster (no weapon, an attunement at 80), Elder / Enmity can swap Shadowcast 20 for Thundercall 20 or go Ironsing 60 + Shadowcast 40, and Mage has an 80 / 80 two-attunement version.
- New "Wanted (either)" stats: list the values you want (for example Strength 40, Agility 65) and the optimizer decides whether each one goes before the shrine (its talents taken early, then the shrine shrinks it) or after it, whichever gets the most talents for the fewest points. It's a new row in the preset window and under Stat floors on the Stats tab, and the results say where each one went.
- The AI build maker treats the numbers you type as wanted stats too, so it picks before or after the shrine for you. Say "before the shrine" or "after" (or "pre" / "post") to choose yourself. Fortitude still goes after the shrine, since your health comes from the final value.
- The AI build maker no longer asks which oath you want when you've already named one, even written as two words ("blade harper", "jet striker", "silent heart") or when your linked build has one.
- Dropdown menus have a cleaner, modern look: rounded, a slim arrow, and a highlight on hover and focus, in every theme.

## v0.35 | 2026-11-17
- Mage builds with no weapon stats now get the Hero's Blade of their strongest element (Flame, Frost, Lightning, Wind or Shadow) automatically. Pick another weapon, or clear it, and your choice stays.
- Presets with a weapon now start at 80 in a weapon of your choice: pick Light, Medium or Heavy with the buttons at the top of the preset window (Elder / Enmity keeps Medium 90 for the Shattered Katana; the mage presets stay weapon-free).

- Loading a preset keeps exactly its stats: "More damage" no longer raises the weapon above what you picked until you press Optimize build.
- 12 hand-picked builds (9 Chime of Conflict builds, weighted highest, and 3 gank builds) now count extra in talent popularity, take rates, recommendations and the AI build maker's oath choice.
- The Chime preset now starts like those builds: Charisma, Strength and Agility 40 before the shrine, Fortitude 50 after, Medium 90.
- New Diluvian preset (replaces Brick Wall Dawnwalker): Dawnwalker with Brick Wall, Fortitude and Willpower 100 before the shrine (75 after), and every talent those two stats unlock, already picked. Your weapon, attunements and the rest are up to you.
- Willpower Tank preset removed (Diluvian covers it).
- Presets only set an oath when they're built around it: Jetstriker Agility, Elder / Enmity (Bladeharper), Bossraid Mage (Saintsworn), Diluvian (Dawnwalker) and a new Silentheart preset. The others leave the oath up to you, and the AI build maker picks the best fit for them.
- Every preset's stats now meet its oath. Bossraid Mage gets 15 in the other four elements that Saintsworn needs; Bossraid, High Strength, High Intelligence, Reinforced Armor, Mage and No Willpower had oaths their stats didn't meet, so they're gone.
- Fixed: picking a weapon that scales with Mind or Body (The Flippers of Fate, Kindred Edict, Black Death, Red Death, The Pastry Paster, Guiding Star, The Long Tong of the Law, Silas's Cenotaph) broke the weapon panel.
- Optimize equipment tries a few starting points and keeps the best, so it lands on the armor targets more often (for example 487 HP / 50% physical instead of 485 / 49%).
- Optimize never ends over the point budget any more (it could finish up to 33 over), and it now also fixes builds whose stat floors alone are over 330 by lowering the floors.
- Optimize and Auto-pick never raise a stat again after the shrine (they used to, e.g. Willpower 40 → 18 → 20 for a talent that needs Strength too), and Fortitude isn't left the same before and after.
- Auto-pick takes back its own picks if they would put the build over budget.
- If a build still can't fit after lowering every floor, Optimize removes picked mantras (and then a picked weapon) as a last resort, and tells you which.
- Talents are blocked when one of their prerequisites conflicts with a path you picked (Eruption Path plus a Twisted Puppets talent used to bring in Wraith Path too). Optimize removes such conflicts.
- Silentheart: talents that need an attunement mantra are blocked, and Optimize removes them.
- Optimize keeps every stat inside the limits you set (the shrine could push a limited stat a little over), and removes any picked talent you've marked as avoided.
- Builds that don't use the Shrine of Order (under Power 8) now import back correctly from the build file.
- Share links open with the same stat plan the sender had.
- Loading a preset no longer fills your talents on its own when "Optimizer favors" isn't Balanced (Bossraid used to start with 76 talents). Press Optimize build to get them.
