## v0.40 | 2026-11-19
- Shrine of Blasphemy: new optimizer setting "Use the Shrine of Blasphemy first". It lets the Shrine of Order be used from Power 5 (90 points) instead of Power 8 (135). Imported builds that use the shrine below 135 points switch it on, and the AI build maker turns it on when you say "blasphemy".
- Fortitude at a talent's level (Concussion needs Fortitude 15) is no longer left the same before and after the shrine. The optimizer tries your three ways out and keeps the best: both stats maxed before the shrine, the other stat moved after the shrine too, or skip those talents and tag Fortitude at 1.
- Will o' Wisp is taken when the build runs two or more Wisps, or when its Wisp has no slot left (support slot taken, no wildcard free), and it adds a Wisp mantra slot.
- After 100 before the shrine, the weapon or attunement isn't pushed past 80 after it (the damage slider, the Optimize focus plans and leftover spending). Those points go to talents or a lower attunement instead. Numbers you type still win.
- Mages (AI build maker mages, the Mage and Bossraid Mage presets) don't get a Hero's Blade automatically, and the AI build maker no longer gives them an oath that needs weapon points (Bladeharper).
- 100/100 talents (Scorching Decay, Night Terror, Frozen Anchor…) are planned like the top builds on deepwoken.co: weapon and attunement 100 before the shrine, everything else tagged at 1, then the weapon stays at the 75 the shrine leaves and the attunement goes to 80. They're no longer raised back to 100 after the shrine (example: a Twisted Puppets + Scorching Decay build went from 0–14 points left to 36).
- A second attunement that's only there for a path (Shadowcast for Twisted Puppets, Frostdraw for steam) is used for the path and isn't raised again after the shrine, unless a mantra you asked for needs it. No more "Shadowcast 45 before, 40 after".
- Adjustments: two or more changes in one line all go through. "more agility and strength", "remove Ghost and Speed Demon", "raise flame and frost by 10", "set agility and willpower to 40", "less flame, more frost". "Remove A and B" used to add B.
- Adjustments understand everyday words: "bump up int", "take out frost", "a heavier weapon", "get rid of Ghost", and "more"/"less" work for attunements too.
- AI build maker understands everyday descriptions: "hard to kill", "doesn't die", "heal my friends", "mostly spells, don't want to M1", "from behind", "zooms around", "destroy armor", "steals life", "big sword", "daggers", "hit really hard" / "max damage" (favors damage), "my M1s" (weapon damage).
- AI build maker: "I don't want any charisma or intelligence" and "I hate charisma builds" now leave them out. They used to add Charisma 75 (and Intelligence 60). "Nothing tanky" means no Fortitude floor (it used to be read as tanky), and "the 100/100 frost talent" works with the slash.
- Auto-pick no longer adds weapon-class talents your weapon can't use (Spear, Club, Rifle, Twinblade talents on a sword build). Compared with your featured builds, auto-pick now picks 76% of the same talents (was 66%).
- Fortitude: when the optimizer needed points before the shrine to reach Power 8 and used Fortitude 50 for it (then 50 again after), it now tags Fortitude at 1 and puts those points into a stat you raise after the shrine anyway.
- No more odd before-shrine numbers left behind by the last steps of optimizing (like Galebreathe 59): a final tidy-up snaps them to a clean level when that costs nothing.
- Smarter optimizing when the budget is tight: before lowering your stats or dropping talents, the optimizer now checks properly whether your required talents and set stats fit (a quick check said "53 over" for a build that fit with points to spare, so it lowered Intelligence 75 and dropped 9 talents). When talents still have to go, it runs one deep search and adds back what the freed points allow.
- Stats you raise after the shrine are tagged at 1 before it when that's cheaper (example: Fortitude 0 → 50 cost 5 more points than tagging it and putting Strength 60 before the shrine).
- A build that wasn't using the Shrine of Order now tries a plan that does, with a stat your oath needs (Saltchemist's Intelligence 75) at 100 before it so the shrine brings it to 75.
- Fixed: an advanced talent whose chain keeps needing a path (Reshape and Remold needs Refine and Reuse, which needs Gilded Path) could be taken next to a path that excludes it (Composite Path: Alloyblood). It's now treated as a conflict.

"flame 50 shadow 40" still works the usual way.

## v0.38 | 2026-11-18
- The AI build maker understands short stat names: "100 flm 100 wep talent" now means Scorching Decay (it used to add every 100/100 talent, or none).
- Charisma follows the Fortitude rule: before the shrine it stays at 1 and goes up after, unless a talent or mantra in your build, your oath or a value you set needs it there. No more Charisma 75 just to drop to 50, or for talents you didn't pick.
- Fixed: pressing an arrow key or the spinner on an empty stat box (one that shows the optimizer's number in grey) started from 0, so one stray click could lock Medium Weapon at 0 and move your weapon points to Heavy. The box now starts from the number it shows.
- If you lock your weapon type low and the build has to carry its weapon points in another type, the results now tell you and how to undo it.
- Advanced talents like Volcanic Glass can now use a path you unroll later: take Eruption, get Volcanic Glass, unroll Eruption and take Twisted Puppets. The talent stays, and the path's stats are still planned.

## v0.37 | 2026-11-18
- Smarter stat placement: the optimizer now puts points where they save the most. A stat goes before the shrine at the level a talent needs (or its full value, like Medium 100 and Flamecharm 100 for a 100/100 talent), or it's tagged at 1 and raised after the shrine. No more odd splits like Medium 66 before the shrine and 100 after.
- The optimizer no longer trades away spare points for stats you didn't ask for. In testing, some builds went from 1 point left to 51 with the same talents (a Bladeharper Twisted Puppets build) and a Saintsworn heavy build from 29 to 116.
- When two plans cost the same, the one that uses the Shrine of Order and takes your required talents before it wins.
- The optimizer also tries every combination of "tagged at 1", "at the target" and "target + 25, so the shrine brings it down to the target" for the stats you set, which covers weapon stats too (Heavy 100 before the shrine lands on 75, so an 80 target needs only 5 more after).
- The AI build maker understands "the 100/100 talents" (100 weapon + 100 attunement): it requires every one for the attunements you named (Scorching Decay, Night Terror, Frozen Anchor, Silencer's Song, Torture Mastery) and plans weapon and attunement at 100. "100 flame 100 weapon" picks just that one.
- With 100/100 talents on two attunements, the AI build maker takes them before the shrine (weapon and both attunements at 100 there) instead of planning 100 / 100 / 100 to the end, which can't fit. Example: an Umbrite Witherblade Bladeharper Twisted Puppets build with Scorching Decay and Night Terror went from 1 point left to 26.
- "Black flames" (or dark / purple flames) means the Twisted Puppets path, and the AI build maker requires the whole path.
- Fortitude is tagged at 1 before the shrine and raised to its target after. It only goes higher before the shrine to take a Fortitude talent there (To The Finish 50, Mythic Stability 85, Reinforced Armor 90). It never goes 75 just to land on 50; that wastes points. The Bossraid presets now do Fortitude 50 after the shrine this way.
- A mantra learned from a path the rest of your build excludes (like Crystal Impale, from the Glass path, in a Steam build) now stays: the builder plans the path's stats so you can take it, learn the mantra and unroll the path, and the results say so.
- Observation and Safety Dance can now stay next to Risky Moves when you pick them or import a build with them: their shop version (Flame Worshipper memento, Power 5) has no conflict. Auto-picks still keep them apart.
- "More Fortitude" / "tankier" in Adjustments now go to the next level that unlocks something (50, then 90 for Reinforced Armor) instead of 75, which unlocks nothing.
- Optimize now searches harder (twice as many starting plans, longer polishing). It can take a few seconds longer, and the Optimize window says so while it works ("Optimizing… this may take a little time"); a popup shows the same when a preset or AI build is being set up.
- "Wanted" stats are now called "Must hit": at one point in the build the stat reaches that level, for its talents, before or after the shrine (the optimizer picks).
- An attribute may go back up a little after the shrine: up to 5 points (like Strength 100 → 75 → 80 for a level), when that's cheaper. The results say when it happens. On the 15 featured builds this cut the talents that couldn't fit from 72 to 36.
- Fixed: a mantra that needs a path your required talents rule out (Cauterize needs Sanguine Blaze, which Lava Serpent excludes) could put both paths in the build. Optimize now removes that mantra.
- Fixed: two required talents that exclude each other (for example Azure Flames and the Sanguine Blaze path) could both end up in the build. The first one stays; Optimize removes the later one and says why.
- Fixed: Fortitude could stay the same before and after the shrine when it had a before-shrine floor.
- Optimizing builds with an impossible wish list (for example two 100/100 talents, Collapsed Lung and Saintsworn together) is faster: floors come down in bigger steps when the build is far over budget.

## v0.36 | 2026-11-17
- Every preset now has variations to pick from, like Attunementless: open a preset and choose a build shell at the top (for example High Strength: Strength 100 + Agility 40, Strength 100 + Agility 65 for Cheap Shot, or Strength 90 + Charisma 40). The first one is the preset as before.
- A variation fills in the stats, Fortitude after the shrine (50, or 90 for Reinforced Armor where it fits), and for some presets the attunements, weapon level and damage focus. Change anything after picking one to make your own version.
- Chime's variations come from the featured Chime of Conflict builds, including a caster version with an attunement at 100 and no weapon.
- Diluvian can now start as a caster (no weapon, an attunement at 80), Elder / Enmity can swap Shadowcast 20 for Thundercall 20 or go Ironsing 60 + Shadowcast 40, and Mage has an 80 / 80 two-attunement version.
- New "Wanted (either)" stats: list the values you want (for example Strength 40, Agility 65) and the optimizer decides whether each one goes before the shrine (its talents taken early, then the shrine shrinks it) or after it, whichever gets the most talents for the fewest points. It's a new row in the preset window and under Stat floors on the Stats tab, and the results say where each one went.
- The AI build maker treats the numbers you type as wanted stats too, so it picks before or after the shrine for you. Say "before the shrine" or "after" (or "pre" / "post") to choose yourself. Fortitude still goes after the shrine, since your health comes from the final value.
- The AI build maker no longer asks which oath you want when you've already named one, even written as two words ("blade harper", "jet striker", "silent heart") or when your linked build has one.
- Dropdown menus have a cleaner, modern look: rounded, a slim arrow, and a highlight on hover and focus, in every theme.

- Auto-pick takes back its own picks if they would put the build over budget.
- If a build still can't fit after lowering every floor, Optimize removes picked mantras (and then a picked weapon) as a last resort, and tells you which.
- Talents are blocked when one of their prerequisites conflicts with a path you picked (Eruption Path plus a Twisted Puppets talent used to bring in Wraith Path too). Optimize removes such conflicts.
- Silentheart: talents that need an attunement mantra are blocked, and Optimize removes them.
- Optimize keeps every stat inside the limits you set (the shrine could push a limited stat a little over), and removes any picked talent you've marked as avoided.
- Builds that don't use the Shrine of Order (under Power 8) now import back correctly from the build file.
- Share links open with the same stat plan the sender had.
- Loading a preset no longer fills your talents on its own when "Optimizer favors" isn't Balanced (Bossraid used to start with 76 talents). Press Optimize build to get them.
