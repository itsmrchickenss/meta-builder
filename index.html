Importing from deepwoken.co

deepwoken.co doesn't let other sites read its builds, so a pasted deepwoken.co link can't be opened here. Copy the build itself instead:

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar (only once): 📋 Copy build for Meta Builder
  2. Open the build on deepwoken.co and click that bookmark. It copies the build.
  3. Come back here and paste it in the box above.

Mantras

Each normal mantra costs 2 talent slots. Oath, monster and event mantras are free.

⚔ True combos

Starter mantras that hold the target (grab, bind or freeze, knock up or down, counter) and the follow‑ups fast enough to land after them, using the mantras this build has or can learn. Timings shift with ping, modifiers and patches, so test them in game and save the ones that work as your own.
